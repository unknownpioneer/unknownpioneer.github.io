I have finished the game (*Orwell: Keeping an Eye on You*). I can't understand the ending...

It's a story about a radical team, "Thought," which has committed an act of terrorism and caused deaths.

At the end of the game, "Thought" is considered a terrorist organization, and most of its members have been arrested.

Personally, although living under a surveillance society is unsuitable for me, we really need some necessary <q>CCTVs</q> to clean up terrorism.
