About this site

This project is a personal website built as a compact archive for thoughts, interests, and digital materials. It is intentionally simple, direct, and easy to maintain.

  • Purpose: A space for writing, media, and personal references.
  • Focus: Blogging, acknowledgements, hobbies, and downloads.
  • Style: Minimal, high-contrast, and intentionally personal.
